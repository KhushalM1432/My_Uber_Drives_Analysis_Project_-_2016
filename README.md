## Uber Trip Patterns & Travel Behavior Analytics | Python

## Data Science

### **Python | Pandas | NumPy | Data Cleaning | EDA | Statistical Analysis | Transportation Analytics**

## Project Overview

Developed a Python-based Uber Trip Analysis project using historical driving records from 2016 to investigate travel behavior, trip frequency, journey purposes, distance distributions, and time-based travel patterns.

Applied data cleaning, preprocessing, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), descriptive statistics, and data visualization to transform raw trip records into meaningful analytical insights relevant to transportation planning and operational performance.

## Business Problem

Transportation and mobility businesses need to understand travel patterns, journey frequency, trip characteristics, and customer mobility behavior to support operational planning.

This project addresses the analytical challenge of examining historical trip records to identify travel trends, understand journey characteristics, and highlight opportunities for improved transportation planning.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze historical Uber driving records from 2016.
- Investigate trip frequency and travel behavior.
- Examine journey purposes and travel categories.
- Evaluate trip distance distributions.
- Identify daily, monthly, and time-based travel patterns.
- Generate statistical visualizations and business-relevant insights.
- Support evidence-based transportation and mobility planning.

## Project Highlights

1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Prepared historical driving records using Python, Pandas, and NumPy by addressing missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent formats, and data quality issues.

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Investigated trip frequency, journey categories, travel purposes, and distance distributions to identify meaningful transportation patterns.

3. **Trip Frequency & Trend Analysis:** Examined historical trip activity across reporting periods to understand variations in travel frequency.

4. **Travel Purpose & Category Analysis:** Analyzed business-related and personal travel categories to understand differences in journey characteristics and mobility behavior.

5. **Trip Distance Analysis:** Explored trip mileage and distance distributions to evaluate travel characteristics and identify unusually long or short journeys.

6. **Time-Based Travel Analysis:** Investigated available trip timestamps to understand daily, monthly, and time-related travel patterns.

7. **Statistical Analysis:** Applied descriptive statistics and distribution analysis to summarize trip characteristics, examine variability, and identify unusual observations.

8. **Data Visualization & Reporting:** Used Matplotlib and Seaborn to communicate trip distributions, travel patterns, and category-level comparisons.

9. **Operational Insights:** Interpreted analytical findings to identify potential applications in trip planning, travel expense monitoring, and mobility operations.

10. **Reproducible Analytical Workflow:** Organized the original dataset, cleaned records, Python notebook, and HTML report to support analytical review and future investigation.
