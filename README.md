## Uber_Drives_Analysis - 2016 | Data_Science & Business_Analytics_Project | Python

## Data_Science

## 🚗Project Highlights

1. **Business Problem Analysis:** Analyzed Uber driving records to understand trip patterns, travel behavior, ride frequency, and potential opportunities to improve operational efficiency.

2. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Used Python, Pandas, and NumPy to clean and prepare the dataset by handling missing values, removing duplicate records, and improving data consistency for reliable analysis.

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Investigated trip frequency, ride purposes, travel categories, trip distances, and time-based patterns to uncover meaningful insights from historical driving data.

4. **Travel Behavior Analytics:** Examined driving patterns across different trip purposes and categories to understand how travel demand varies across personal and business-related activities.

5. **Statistical & Descriptive Analysis:** Applied descriptive statistics and distribution analysis to summarize trip characteristics, identify unusual observations, and understand variations in travel activity.

6. **Feature Analysis & Pattern Identification:** Explored available dataset attributes, including trip duration, distance, purpose, and travel timing where recorded, to identify patterns relevant to transportation and operational planning.

7. **Data Visualization & Reporting:** Used Matplotlib and Seaborn to create visualizations that communicate trip trends, category distributions, and travel patterns in an accessible, business-oriented format.

8. **Business Insight Generation:** Translated analytical findings into observations that can support travel expense monitoring, trip planning, utilization analysis, and operational decision-making.

9. **Data Quality & Reliability:** Applied data validation and preprocessing techniques to improve the consistency of analytical outputs and establish a reliable foundation for further analysis.

10. **Data-Driven Decision Support:** Organized analytical findings into a structured workflow that helps stakeholders understand historical travel activity and identify areas for further operational investigation.

11. **Analytical Problem-Solving:** Applied a systematic data science process, from data preparation and exploratory analysis to visualization and interpretation, to investigate a real-world transportation-related dataset.

12. **Reproducible Data Workflow:** Saved the cleaned dataset and organized analytical outputs to support subsequent analysis, reporting, and visualization.

## Tools & Technical Skills

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Data Cleaning, Missing Value Imputation, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Descriptive Statistics, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Data Visualization, Pattern Analysis, Business Analytics, and Data-Driven Decision-Making.

## Project Objective

To transform historical Uber driving records into meaningful analytical insights by examining travel patterns, trip characteristics, and ride frequency, supporting evidence-based operational analysis and data-driven planning.
